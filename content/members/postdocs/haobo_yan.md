I am Haobo Yan (燕浩波), a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Bern. I received my Ph.D. from Peking University in 2026 with the thesis [*Multi-Hadron Scattering from Lattice Quantum Chromodynamics*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12508). I was previously a visitor at UC Berkeley and Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (2019–2020) and at the University of Bonn (2024–2025).

My research uses lattice quantum chromodynamics (lattice QCD) to study hadron interactions from first principles. Recently, I have been working on multi-hadron scattering and resonances, from mesons such as the ω(782) and π(1300) to baryon–baryon systems such as ΞΞ.
